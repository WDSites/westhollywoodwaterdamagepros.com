Water damage in West Hollywood, CA usually needs more than one kind of work. Water has to be stopped and pumped out, the structure has to be dried, debris has to be cleaned up and the building has to be rebuilt. Each service below covers one of those stages, and a single call to the 24/7 line starts whichever one you need first.

If you are not sure where to begin, the quick rule is this. Standing water is removal, and wet walls and floors are mitigation. Soaked or dirty materials are cleanup, and a stain or soft board after drying is repair. Floods or sewage are their own category. Mold sits on the end of the chain when moisture lingered.

Why local buildings change the plan

Most homes in West Hollywood are small houses and duplexes from the 1920s, with some seven and eight story apartment buildings from the 1920s and 1930s mixed in. Many buildings predate 1993, so older supply lines, cast iron drains and layered finishes are common, and about four in five households rent. A leak in one unit often reaches the unit below, which is why the order of services matters.

Permits also shape the work. The City of West Hollywood says repairing a leak in water piping is exempt from a permit, while replacing drain, soil, waste or vent piping needs one from Building and Safety on the second floor of City Hall at 8300 Santa Monica Blvd. Ask whoever fixes the plumbing which side of that line the job falls on.

How the services fit together

Mold remediation can enter at any point after the second step when moisture lingered longer than a day or two. Flood and sewage jobs add a safety step at the start and a sanitizing step in the middle.

What this network does not do

Restoration is not plumbing repair, roofing or waterproofing. A plumber repairs the failed pipe, a roofer repairs a roof leak and a waterproofing specialist addresses a foundation that lets water in repeatedly. Restoration pays for itself when those problems are fixed first, so the crew will tell you when another trade is needed before drying begins.

Fire and smoke damage are not part of this site's services. The crews here handle water, flood and mold only.
