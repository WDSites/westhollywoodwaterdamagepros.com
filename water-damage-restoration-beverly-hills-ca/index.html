If water is still spreading, do this first

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the building's main shut-off. If you cannot reach it, ask the property manager or the water utility.

Power and safety

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can do it from a dry spot. If water touches outlets, the panel or gas appliances, leave and call 911.

Lift valuables

Move electronics, papers, rugs and small furniture onto dry surfaces. Do not lift heavy wet furniture alone.

Protect people and pets

Keep children and pets out of standing water, especially if it may be dirty or near electrical equipment.

Take photos

Photograph the source, the water level and damaged items before anything is moved. Your insurer or property manager will ask.

Beverly Hills local note

Beverly Hills water, permits and who to call

Beverly Hills Water is a city service run through Public Works. The City of West Hollywood notes that Beverly Hills Water also serves certain areas on the west side of West Hollywood, so addresses near the border can be served by either city. Check a recent water bill to see which utility supplies yours, since that utility owns the street valve and the meter.

For permits after a repair, the Development Services Division Permit Center, commonly known as Building and Safety, is at 455 N. Rexford Dr., 1st Floor, Beverly Hills, CA 90210, and it schedules appointments. Beverly Hills and West Hollywood together are entirely surrounded by the City of Los Angeles, which is why hillside and border addresses can fall under three different city agencies.
