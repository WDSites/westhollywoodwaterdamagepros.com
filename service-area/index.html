Our water damage and mold service area is West Hollywood, CA and the neighboring city of Beverly Hills. Both are small cities packed with apartments, hillside homes and commercial blocks, and together they are surrounded by the City of Los Angeles. That geography explains most coverage questions, since a block on either side of a border can answer to a different utility or permit office.

The 24/7 dispatch line works for the whole area. Call and give the street address, and the dispatcher confirms the city, the nearest cross street and what to do while a crew is sent.

How dispatch works across both cities

Two different water utilities and two different permit counters apply, so the first call sorts out the city. LADWP serves most areas of West Hollywood and Beverly Hills Water serves certain west side areas, according to the city. West Hollywood provides its own sewer service, and Beverly Hills handles permits at the Permit Center at 455 N. Rexford Dr.

Hillside streets, narrow lots and subterranean parking all affect how a crew stages pumps and drying equipment. Tell the dispatcher about gated entries, garage levels and whether an elevator or loading zone is available, because those details decide how fast equipment gets to the wet area.

West Hollywood

The core of the service area is the City of West Hollywood, with the primary ZIP code 90069 covering the Sunset Strip corridor. The city also shares mailing ZIP codes with neighboring Los Angeles neighborhoods, including 90046 and 90048, so a West Hollywood address can have a different ZIP than the one on a neighbor's mail. Coverage follows the city limits and not the ZIP code.

Within the city, calls come from the Sunset Strip, the Norma Triangle, West Hollywood West, West Hollywood East and West Hollywood North, and from the Santa Monica Boulevard corridor that runs through the middle. See the West Hollywood home page for services and local detail.

Beverly Hills

Beverly Hills borders West Hollywood to the west. We serve ZIP codes 90210, 90211 and 90212 there, and the Beverly Hills page explains the local water utility, the permit counter and the difference between the city and parts of Los Angeles that share a Beverly Hills mailing address.

Neighboring Los Angeles areas

Hollywood, the Fairfax District, Beverly Grove and the Hollywood Hills border the service area. They are parts of the City of Los Angeles, and coverage there is best confirmed by phone, since it depends on the street. If your address is close to the border, call and ask.

A crew is dispatched when one is available. No arrival time is promised.

Multi-unit buildings add one extra step. The dispatcher asks for the property manager's name or building engineer so access to stairs, garages and shut-offs can be arranged ahead of the crew.

Before you call

  • Have the street address and the nearest cross street ready.
  • Know whether the building is a house, a condo or a rental, and who manages it.
  • Take photos of the water and the source if it is safe to do so.
  • Note whether the water is clean, dirty or from a sewer or drain.
  • Check your policy for the claim phone number and any pre-approval rules.
