Why West Hollywood is its own kind of job

The city is small and dense, with a Census count of about 35,757 people in 2020 and roughly four in five households renting. That mix means leaks cross unit lines, shared walls hide wet insulation and the party that pays depends on who owns the pipe. A good scope names the source, the rooms affected and who needs to be told.

The city is hilly in the north, with grades near 6 percent, and flatter in the south, where the grade is closer to 2 percent. The western part of the city was marsh before the 1920s, and the city's hazard plan notes it is susceptible to liquefaction. Flooding ranks last of 12 hazards in that plan, so most water damage here comes from plumbing, roofs and water main breaks, not rivers.

In 2026 a 36-inch LADWP water main broke at Sunset Blvd and Holloway Dr and sent about 17 million gallons across the area, closing Sunset for more than a week and flooding garages and subterranean parking, according to local news reports. The cause is under investigation. It is a reminder that even a careful owner can face water from outside the property line.

Standards we point to

Restoration has published industry standards, and they shape how the work is described on this site. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration, including the three water categories and four classes of water damage. ANSI/IICRC S520 covers mold remediation. You are encouraged to ask any company whether its crews work to these standards.

Nothing here claims an individual certification for a person or a firm, and no license number is printed because crews are dispatched from the network. For the crew sent to you, ask for a license number and check it at the Contractors State License Board website, cslb.ca.gov.

What we do not do

We do not repair plumbing, fix roofs or waterproof foundations. If your water problem comes from a failed pipe, a roof leak or groundwater entering repeatedly, a plumber, roofer or waterproofing specialist needs to fix the source before drying and repair can last.

We do not handle fire or smoke damage. We do not rent drying equipment to homeowners, because moisture readings are what end a drying job and they come with the crew. We do not print review quotes, star ratings or customer counts on this site.

How to verify a contractor in California

  • Ask for the CSLB license number and check it at cslb.ca.gov. California requires a license for jobs totaling $1,000 or more in labor and materials.
  • Get a written contract with the scope, the price and the payment schedule.
  • Do not pay more than 10 percent down or $1,000, whichever is less.
  • Ask whether your insurer needs to inspect the damage before repairs begin.
  • Keep photos, moisture readings and receipts.

That checklist follows guidance published by the Contractors State License Board and the Los Angeles County Department of Consumer and Business Affairs. It applies to any restoration company, ours included.
